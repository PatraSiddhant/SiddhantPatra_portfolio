CampusSwipe — Campus Discovery, Rebuilt

Built a mobile-first event discovery app that turns Columbia Business School's CampusGroups ICS feed into a swipe experience inspired by consumer apps. 05 / 07

Sphere Wrapped — Solving the "Phone Paradox"

For a product innovation project focused on the Las Vegas Sphere, our team studied a clear behavioral conflict: guests pay for total immersion, but many spend 30 to 40 percent of the show filming. We framed this as "Memory FOMO vs. Real-Time Awe" and proposed Sphere Wrapped, a post-show product that gives guests personalized recap reels generated from venue infrastructure. The concept combines seat-perspective matching, candid reaction captures, and social-ready delivery so guests can stay present during the event without sacrificing digital memories.
